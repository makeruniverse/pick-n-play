Pulling the plug never breaks anything. It is always allowed.

## Something looks wrong on the screen, or the music is gone

Hold **all four buttons** at the same time for **5 seconds**.
The screen goes dark. The game comes back by itself within a minute.

## The light strip glows calm orange

The game is restarting by itself. Wait 2 minutes.
Still orange after that? Go to the next step.

## Still not working, or the arm doesn't follow

1. Pull the **power plug**.
2. Wait **10 seconds**.
3. Plug it back in.
4. Wait **2 minutes**. Everything starts by itself. Don't press anything.

## The arm moves strangely or hits something

Pull the **power plug right away.** Then leave it unplugged.
